About Mooots

Mooots is a social meetup app designed to help people connect in real life through spontaneous local hangouts and shared experiences.

We're building a space where meeting new people feels natural, whether you're new to the area, looking to make friends, or simply want to get out and do something different.

Less scrolling. More living.

How Mooots Works

01

Discover Local Hangouts

Explore gatherings happening around you and find opportunities to connect with people in your community.

02

Create Your Own Mooot

Have an idea for a hangout? Create a Mooot, choose a location, and invite others to join.

03

Meet in Real Life

Turn online connections into real-world experiences, meet new people, and build friendships beyond the screen.
