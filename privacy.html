Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Mooots is a brand operated by Third Place Ventures LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Mooots mobile app, the mooots.app website and our waitlist.

Mooots is built so you can meet people without giving away who you are: your profile is a handle and a spirit animal, never your real name or face. This policy explains what we store, why, and who can see it.

The Mooots app

What we collect

Only you can see: your email address (for signing in and account recovery) and your phone number, if you add one (for recovery and verification).

People on Mooots can see your profile: handle, spirit animal, color, bio, member-since date, roll photos, and whether you're verified.

People in a mooot can see that you hosted or joined it, and the notes, replies, photos and videos you post there.

You and the other person can see your friends, friend requests and invites. Blocks and reports are seen only by you and our review team.

We don't collect your contacts, and we don't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

Your location

With your permission, Mooots uses your phone's location to show mooots near you and how far away they are. We don't store your location on our servers, and it's never shown to other people.

When you host, the meeting spot you choose is shared with the people who can see that mooot.

Who sees your profile

Other people see your handle, spirit animal, color, bio and roll photos, and whether you're verified. They never see your email, phone number or real name.

You can block anyone: blocked people can't see your profile or your posts, and you won't see theirs.

Identity verification

Verification is optional. Our verification partner, Stripe Identity, checks your ID and a selfie. Mooots receives only the result (verified, and whether you're 18+ or 21+). We never receive or store your ID, your selfie, or the name on your ID.

Who we share with

We don't sell your information. We use Google Firebase to store data and run the app, and Stripe Identity for ID checks; they process data only on our behalf. We may share information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Keeping your data

A mooot's chat and photos stay available to its members after it ends, as a keepsake. We keep them for 12 months after the mooot ends, then delete them. You can delete your own posts and photos any time before then.

Account Deletion and Safety Reports

You may request deletion of your Mooots account through the app. Upon deletion, we remove your account, public profile, profile photos, and associated account information, subject to limited exceptions.

This includes your sign-in account, your profile and roll photos, your friends, requests, invites and blocks, and the notes, replies, photos and videos you posted in mooots. Your handle becomes available again. Upcoming mooots you host are cancelled, and the people who joined are told. Mooots you hosted that already happened stay for the people who went, with the host shown as "Deleted account". We ask Stripe to delete your ID check.

We may retain information contained in safety and moderation reports when reasonably necessary to investigate misconduct, protect our community, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations. Account identifiers are removed or anonymized where appropriate.

Closed moderation reports are ordinarily retained for 90 days after resolution, unless additional retention is necessary for an ongoing investigation, legal obligation, or related dispute.

Your choices

  • Turn location on or off in your phone's settings
  • Block or report anyone
  • Delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account), or by emailing contact@mooots.app
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to delete it, at contact@mooots.app

The website and waitlist

What we collect

Your phone number, if you join the waitlist. That's the only thing we ask you for.

A record of your consent: the date and time you joined and the wording you agreed to. Texting laws require us to keep this.

Which link brought you here, like the one in our TikTok bio, so we know where people hear about us. We read this from the link itself, not from cookies or tracking.

Basic technical info that any website receives when you visit, like your IP address and browser type. Our hosting provider keeps this in short-term logs to run and secure the site.

We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or tracking pixels.

How we use it

We use your phone number only to send you one text when it's your turn to get the Mooots app. If you reply STOP, we'll send one final message confirming you're unsubscribed. We won't send marketing from anyone else, and we won't use your number for anything unrelated.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services that help us run the waitlist:

  • GitHub, which hosts this website
  • Google, which stores the waitlist (in Google Sheets)
  • A text messaging provider, which will deliver your invite. We'll name it here before we send any texts.

These providers can use your information only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your number until we've sent your invite, you opt out, or you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. We may keep the consent record for as long as the law requires.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any text to stop getting them. Reply HELP for help. To have your number deleted from the waitlist, email us at contact@mooots.app and we'll remove it.

For everyone

Age

Mooots and its waitlist are for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If we learn someone under 18 has an account or has joined the waitlist, we delete it.

Your California privacy rights

If you live in California, you have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to get a copy of it, to correct it, and to have it deleted. We don't sell or share your personal information for advertising.

To use any of these rights, email contact@mooots.app. We'll confirm it's you before acting, and we won't treat you differently for asking.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date. If a change matters, we'll tell you in the app first. We won't use your waitlist number in a new way without asking you first.

Contact

Questions? Email contact@mooots.app.
